# Incident response

## First response

1. Confirm the signal and open an incident record.
2. Assign an incident lead and communication owner.
3. Contain impact without destroying evidence.
4. Preserve relevant logs with personal data controls.
5. Recover service and verify interview integrity.

## Follow-up

Document the timeline, user impact, root cause, contributing conditions, corrective actions, and owners. Security incidents may require private notification and legal review; do not discuss sensitive details in public issues.
